AI in the Workflow

Three agents doing named jobs, not generic AI

Each one triggers on something specific, decides something specific, and acts.

📊

Deal Risk Agent

Triggers on: a deal's forecast category and close date drifting out of sync across any of the three hardware lines.
Decides: whether the drift is material enough to flag before the next forecast call.
Acts: posts a Slack alert to the deal owner with the specific field that moved and a suggested next step.
🔀

Partner Triage Agent

Triggers on: a technical question posted in a partner-facing Slack channel.
Decides: whether it's a known-spec question or something genuinely novel.
Acts: answers known questions directly; escalates novel ones to engineering with full context attached.
📝

Quote Assembly Agent

Triggers on: a new opportunity reaching the configuration stage.
Decides: which prior similar configuration and pricing pattern best fits the new deal.
Acts: drafts a first-pass quote for the rep to review and send, not start from a blank sheet.
